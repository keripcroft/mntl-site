That conversation happens to all of us, every single day. Before the hard conversation, before you raise your prices, before you say the thing out loud in the meeting, before you close the laptop and actually go be present with your kids.

You're always doing reps.

Every time you push through, that's a rep. Every time you negotiate yourself out of it, that's a rep too. The only question is which ones you're stacking. MNTL is where we stack the right ones. On purpose. Together.

What is Mental Athleticism™
Anchor to an audacious vision, relentlessly execute small daily reps, and consistently pursue internal command and environmental design.

Anchored Vision

Know where you're going, and keep coming back to it when life gets loud.

Relentless Execution

Turn the big vision into small, doable daily actions. Then do them again.

Internal Command

Notice the thoughts and feelings driving the bus, and take the wheel back.

Environmental Design

Build the people, spaces and routines that make the right move the easy move.

What if I can't make the live calls?

Every locker room is recorded and posted in our private community, usually within 24 hours. Live is better (that's where the magic is), but you won't fall behind.

How much time does it take?

About 2 hours a week. One hour live plus 10–15 minutes a day on your rep.

Is this a course?

No. There's no library of 40 videos you'll never watch. It's live training, one rep at a time, with real accountability.

Do I have to own a business?

Nope. MNTL is for founders, corporate folks, parents and anyone else who wants to be more mentally athletic.

How is this different from BYLD?

BYLD is my community for women actively building businesses. MNTL is focused training in Mental Athleticism™, open to anyone. Lots of people will do both. You don't have to.

No. MNTL is mental performance training, not therapy, counseling or medical care. If you need clinical support, please get it. You can absolutely do both.
